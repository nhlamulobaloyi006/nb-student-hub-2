# Lean Business Canvas — NB Student Hub

## 1. Problem

- 60% of SA matriculants don't qualify for their first-choice course
- Learners have no single place to answer course + funding + application + rejection questions
- Most have never seen an APS breakdown of their own marks
- SA has one of the world's highest youth unemployment rates — education access is the bottleneck

## 2. Customer segments

- **Primary:** Grade 12 learners (matric) and their parents
- **Secondary:** first-year tertiary students navigating funding and wellness
- **Tertiary:** schools, NGOs, government departments (DBE, DHET)

## 3. Unique value proposition

**"Know your options. Plan your future."**

The only SA app that handles the entire post-matric journey — including what to do when you don't qualify.

## 4. Solution

A single installable PWA delivering:

- Course matching by APS + subject requirements
- Funding discovery (NSFAS, bursaries, loans, SETAs)
- Application tracking
- Wellness check-ins and study streaks
- A rule-based AI companion (NBOT)
- Matric Upgrade pathways for rejected learners

## 5. Channels

- Word of mouth through schools
- TikTok / Instagram Reels for learners
- WhatsApp share links
- National school partnership program
- DBE partnership for curriculum integration

## 6. Revenue streams

| Stream | Model |
|---|---|
| Freemium | Free for learners |
| Premium (schools) | Bulk accounts + counsellor dashboards |
| Institutional partnerships | Universities pay for placement analytics |
| Government contracts | DBE / DHET integration |

## 7. Cost structure

- **Dev:** solo-built during hackathon
- **Hosting:** free tier (Vercel)
- **Data:** no servers — everything on-device
- **Marketing:** organic + partnerships
- **Growth:** content + school onboarding
- **First-year run-rate:** under R100k total

## 8. Key metrics

- Daily active learners
- Courses applied to per user
- Funding save rate
- 7-day and 30-day streak retention
- PWA install rate
- Return rate after a rejection

## 9. Unfair advantage

The **rejection-to-recovery loop** — the only SA app that turns "you don't qualify" into a real upgrade path. Data moat: every rejection improves upgrade recommendations.

## 10. First 1,000 users

- TikTok demos (organic + boosted)
- School counsellor partnerships in Gauteng
- Paid micro-influencer campaign for matric season (Sep–Nov)
- NSFAS deadline-aligned push notifications
